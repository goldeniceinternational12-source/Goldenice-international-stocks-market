Introduction

Goldenice International respects the privacy of visitors who use our website and contact our team. This Privacy Policy explains how information voluntarily provided through our website, email and communication channels may be handled.

This policy applies to information submitted through Goldenice International's website and related communication channels. It does not replace the privacy policies of third-party platforms that may be linked from this website.

By using this website, you acknowledge that you have read this Privacy Policy.

02

Information We Receive

Because this website is designed without a traditional account or database system, we do not require visitors to create an online account simply to browse the website.

Information may be provided voluntarily when you:

  • Send an enquiry through the contact form.
  • Contact us through WhatsApp.
  • Contact us by email.
  • Communicate with our team regarding an enquiry, investment-related question or service.
03

How We Use Information

Information voluntarily provided to Goldenice International may be used to respond to enquiries, communicate with prospective clients or investors, provide requested information and improve the website experience.

Information may also be used to understand enquiries, provide customer support and communicate information relevant to a visitor's request.

We do not state or imply that information submitted through this website is used to make automated investment decisions about an individual.

04

WhatsApp & Email Communications

This website uses links that allow visitors to contact Goldenice International through WhatsApp or email.

When you choose to use these services, your communication is handled through the relevant third-party platform and is subject to that platform's own privacy practices and terms.

IMPORTANT

Do not send passwords, banking credentials, payment card details, private keys or other highly sensitive security information through ordinary website enquiries or WhatsApp messages.

The website may use cookies or similar technologies in the future to improve functionality, understand website traffic and measure the effectiveness of our digital presence.

Goldenice International will not ask you to disclose passwords, private keys or other confidential authentication credentials through ordinary website enquiries.
