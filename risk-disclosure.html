Investments involve risk.

The value of investments can rise or fall, and investors may lose some or all of the money they invest. Past performance is not a guarantee of future results.

RISK DISCLOSURE

Know the Risks.

Before making an investment decision, prospective investors should understand the risks associated with financial markets and investment products.

01

Market Risk

Financial markets can be volatile. Share prices and other investment values may move up or down because of economic conditions, company performance, interest rates, political events and other factors.

02

Loss of Capital

There is no guarantee that an investor will recover the amount invested. Depending on the investment and market conditions, investors may experience partial or significant losses.

03

Volatility

Market prices can change rapidly. Short-term movements may be unpredictable and can affect the value of an investment significantly.

04

Economic Conditions

Inflation, interest rates, recessions, currency movements and other economic developments may affect financial markets and investment performance.

05

Company & Sector Risk

Individual companies and sectors may experience financial, operational, regulatory or competitive challenges that can affect investment values.

06

Currency Risk

Investors dealing with international investments may be affected by changes in exchange rates between currencies.

07

Liquidity Risk

Some investments may not always be immediately convertible into cash at the desired price. Market conditions can affect the ability to buy or sell.

08

Regulatory Risk

Changes in laws, regulations, tax rules or government policies may affect investment activities and outcomes.

INVESTOR RESPONSIBILITY

Make Informed Decisions.

Every investor has different financial circumstances, objectives, experience and tolerance for risk.

Before making an investment decision, you should carefully consider whether the investment is suitable for you.

Where appropriate, consider obtaining independent financial, legal or tax advice from a qualified professional.

Do not invest money that you cannot afford to lose.
